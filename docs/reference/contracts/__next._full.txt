<p><strong>Names</strong> are camelCase letters and digits starting lowercase, and can't be reserved words in Swift, Kotlin or TypeScript (like <code>default</code> or <code>class</code>).</p>
<p><strong>Colors</strong> are <code>#RRGGBB</code> or <code>#RRGGBBAA</code>. <code>dark</code> is optional and defaults to <code>light</code>. These colors are required: <code>background</code>, <code>onBackground</code>, <code>surface</code>, <code>onSurface</code>, <code>surfaceVariant</code>, <code>onSurfaceVariant</code>, <code>primary</code>, <code>onPrimary</code>, <code>outline</code>, <code>error</code> and <code>onError</code>. You can add more.</p>
<p><strong>Spacing and radius</strong> values are numbers of points (iOS), dp (Android) or pixels (web). A radius of 9999 or more is fully rounded on the web.</p>
